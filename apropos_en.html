The Gothic Tarot 2026 Project

Designed, developed and published by Cyril Darbost, Gothic Tarot 2026 was born from the desire to offer a traditional card game with a strong, dark and polished visual identity, while ensuring flawless technical performance on smartphones and tablets.

The project combines a fluid solo game engine against artificial intelligence, a real-time multiplayer system, and an immersive user experience designed for tarot enthusiasts.

Tarot 2026 is a social tarot game for Android: an Android mobile app for smartphones and tablets, available for free on Google Play.

Creator and publisher

Gothic Tarot 2026 is entirely created, developed and published by Cyril Darbost. For any question, suggestion or bug report: babylone.ch@gmail.com or cyrildarbost@gmail.com.

A mobile game app built for stability

Gothic Tarot 2026 is built with Flutter and relies on Supabase for accounts, scores and real-time multiplayer tables. The app runs on Android smartphones and tablets, offers fifteen languages and is going through a closed beta on Google Play to make online games reliable.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the app free?

Yes, it is free to download and to play. Online tables use chips you earn by playing; chips and cosmetic items are also available as optional in-app purchases.

Which devices can I play on?

Gothic Tarot is a mobile-only game: it installs on Android smartphones and tablets from Google Play.

How can I contact the developer?

Write to Cyril Darbost: babylone.ch@gmail.com or cyrildarbost@gmail.com. You can also chat with other players on the community Discord server.
