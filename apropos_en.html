The Gothic Tarot 2026 Project

Conceived and developed by Cyril Darbost, Gothic Tarot 2026 was born from the desire to offer a traditional card game with a strong, dark, and polished visual identity, while ensuring flawless technical performance on mobile and web.

The project combines a fluid solo game engine against artificial intelligence, a real-time multiplayer system, and an immersive user experience designed for tarot enthusiasts.

Gothic Tarot is an Android mobile app for smartphones and tablets, available for free on Google Play.

An app built for stability

Gothic Tarot 2026 is built with Flutter and relies on Supabase for accounts, scores and real-time multiplayer tables. The app runs on Android smartphones and tablets, offers eight languages and is going through a closed beta on Google Play to make online games reliable.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the app free?
Yes, Gothic Tarot is 100% free to access for all tables and game modes.

Is the app compatible with mobile & PC?
Absolutely, the solo mode against artificial intelligence and the Android app work hand in hand with the website.

Where to contact the developer?
For any suggestions or bug reports, you can use the official GitHub repository of the project or the community Discord server.
