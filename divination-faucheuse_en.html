In Gothic Tarot 2026, victory does not end at the scoreboard. A mystery feature awaits the best player at the table: the Reaper's Divination, a fortune-telling tarot reading that no one else will see.

We will not reveal everything here: the surprise is part of the game. Here is simply what you need to know to have a chance of experiencing it.

The essentials

  • Reserved for the winner: only the player who finishes first in the final total is entitled to it.
  • Online only: it is earned at an online multiplayer table or in a tournament, with 3 to 5 players.
  • Never after a defeat: second place, even by a single point, is not enough.
  • A personal reading: the Reaper reads the arcana for you alone.
  • Free: it is part of the game, with no purchase or option to unlock.

How do you unlock the Reaper's reading?

The condition is simple to state, much harder to meet:

  • Join an online game or a tournament, with 3, 4 or 5 players. Offline games against the AI do not give access to the divination.
  • Play the game to the end. What counts is the final total of all the rounds, not a single successful deal.
  • Finish in first place. The scoreboard appears, and if you are in the lead, the Reaper makes itself known.

In other words, you have to beat real opponents. To give yourself every chance, brush up on the official tarot rules and practise against the game's AI before playing online.

What happens when you win?

We are keeping it secret. Let's just say the Reaper does not merely congratulate you: it opens the doors of its domain and takes the time to read your destiny in the tarot arcana.

The moment has been designed as a true gothic staging, in the game's dark world. What the cards tell you, only you will know.

More personal predictions

In your profile, the Fortune-telling tarot section lets you enter, if you wish, a few personal details for personalised predictions. They are optional and stay on your phone: they are not sent online.

A surprise to enjoy with a smile

The Reaper's Divination is entertainment that extends the game's atmosphere. Its predictions have no real value and replace no advice, whether about love, money or health.

Frequently asked questions about the Reaper's Divination

Can I get the Reaper's reading by playing alone against the AI?

No. The divination is reserved for the winner of an online game or a tournament, with 3 to 5 players. Offline games are for practice.

Do I need to win every round?

No. Only the ranking on the game's final total counts: you must finish first.

Does the Reaper's Divination cost anything?

No. It is part of Gothic Tarot 2026, available on Google Play.

Is my profile information shared?

No. The optional details in the Fortune-telling tarot section are stored on your phone.
