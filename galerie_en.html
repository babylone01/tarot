Visual Immersion in Our Tarot Game

Explore the visuals of this free gothic tarot game before checking our game rules.

These screenshots come from the Gothic Tarot 2026 Android mobile app, on smartphone and tablet. They show the game's visual world, a real five-player game and the weekend tournament screen.

Two more screenshots show a trick in progress and the slot machine where you stake your tokens. The cards follow the traditional 78-card French tarot: 21 trumps, the Excuse and four suits of fourteen cards, from 1 to King. To understand what is happening on the table, read the rules of tarot for 3, 4 and 5 players.

1. Title screen: the Reaper and his cards

The title screen sets the mood: the Reaper shows three tarot cards, including the Petit and the 21 of trumps, two of the three Bouts.

2. Gothic hall and tarot cards

Artwork of the game hall: stained glass, a crystal ball and cards spread on the altar, in the game's blue and gold tones.

3. 5-player game in progress

A round in progress: the five players' avatars surround the table, while the contract and the taker's points appear at the top left. Empty seats are filled by the AI, so you can start a five-player game on your own.

4. Weekend tournament

The weekend tournament runs from Friday to Sunday, with a live player ranking. At the end of Friday's qualifiers, each player is given a category (Green, Yellow or Red) based on their win rate, and the level of the bots at their table adapts to it.

5. A contested trick on the gothic table

Close-up of a trick during a Petite: the cards played overlap in the middle of the pentacle-decorated table, against the lights of a city at night. At the top left, the game shows the contract and the round's points tally, updated after every trick.

6. Slot machine and tokens

Between games, the slot machine spins reels illustrated with tarot cards. For a 50-token stake, three 21s of trumps pay the 12,500-token jackpot, and three Excuses pay 4,000. Winnings are paid in in-game tokens.
