How to play Gothic Tarot

Gothic Tarot follows traditional French tarot: 78 cards, bidding at the start of each round, then a taker who takes on the defence trick after trick. The idea takes minutes to grasp and many games to master: make your contract by winning the right cards at the right time.

The game counts the points for you and shows the score table at the end of every round, so you can focus on strategy rather than arithmetic.

Card values, Bouts and the points needed for each contract are all detailed on the official French tarot rules page.

Tarot for 3, 4 and 5 players

  • 3 players: a fast-paced game where everyone plays for themselves.
  • 4 players: the reference format: a lone taker against three defenders.
  • 5 players: calling the king creates a secret alliance the other players must unmask.

The gothic variant

Gothic Tarot adds two house rules, around the called king and the Excuse played at the end of the round, for even more tactical games. They are explained in full with the game rules.
