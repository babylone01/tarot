This policy explains what data Tarot Gothique 2026 processes, why, with whom, for how long, and how to exercise your rights. It applies to the app (Android, Windows and web version) and to this website. Last updated: 12 November 2026.

1. Data controller

Cyril Darbost — for any question or request: cyrildarbost@gmail.com.

2. Data sent to our servers

  • Account (online play): identifier, email address and, with Google sign-in, the account's name and photo.
  • Profile: nickname, avatar, gender, region and city if you enter them, photos you add.
  • Games: tables, scores, competition (rounds played and won), chip balance and transactions, purchases made through Google Play.
  • Interactions: chat messages, invitations, friends list and online presence (visible to your friends), reports.
  • Technical data: error and crash reports (error message and stack trace, device or browser model and system, app version, account identifier if you are signed in) and problems you report from the settings.
  • Advertising: the Google AdMob SDK may collect the device's advertising ID, the IP address (approximate location) and usage and crash data. In Europe, Google asks for your choice at launch; you can change it at any time with the app's ad choices button.
  • Acceptance of the terms of use and of this policy (date and version).

Offline mode sends no game data.

3. Data that stays on your device

Never sent: your settings, the memory of the app's opponents (your playing habits observed during games), the name, city and date of birth entered for divination tarot, video and ad counters, and your sign-in session. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, erases them.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Running online play (tables, rankings, chat, friends, profile), managing chips and purchases: performance of the service you use.
  • Moderation, security, fraud prevention and bug fixing: the publisher's legitimate interest.
  • Personalised advertising: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Your data is never sold.

5. Security and encryption

All exchanges between the app and the server are encrypted (HTTPS / TLS). Data is hosted by Supabase, which encrypts it at rest. Server-enforced access rules prevent a player from reading or changing another player's data. With Google sign-in, the app never sees your password; with a password, it is sent encrypted and only its protected (hashed) form is stored. Sensitive operations (chips, bonuses, purchases, competition) are checked by the server.

6. Sharing and service providers

Only with the providers the service needs: Supabase (hosting and database), Google (sign-in, Google Play, AdMob advertising), Cloudflare (anti-bot check at sign-in) and Resend (sending error alerts to the developer). Some of them process data outside the European Union, with the safeguards required by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses). Your nickname, avatar and public profile are visible to other online players.

7. Retention

  • Account, profile and game data: as long as your account is active.
  • Error reports: 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • After account deletion: only proof of your purchases is kept, without your identity, plus an unreadable fingerprint of your email that prevents the welcome bonus from being granted twice to the same address.

8. Account deletion

In the app: “My account” window, account deletion button; deletion is immediate. Without access to the app: follow the account deletion page; requests are handled within 72 hours.

9. Your rights (GDPR)

You can access, correct, erase, object to the use of, retrieve your data or withdraw your consent by writing to cyrildarbost@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: www.cnil.fr).

10. Minors

Online play is reserved for players aged 15 and over, or with a parent's permission.

11. This website

This website uses no cookies and no audience measurement tool. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which technically processes visitors' IP addresses to serve the pages and keep them secure.
