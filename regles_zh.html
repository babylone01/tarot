Official French Tarot Rules

French Tarot is played with a 78-card deck consisting of 21 Trumps, The Fool, and 4 traditional suits. Learn more about our project development on our About page.

Oudlers (Bouts) & Required Points

Oudlers OwnedTarget Points for Taker
3 Oudlers (1, 21, Fool)36 points
2 Oudlers41 points
1 Oudler51 points
0 Oudlers56 points

Bidding & Variants (3, 4, and 5 Players)

Bidding options include Push, Guard, Guard Without, and Guard Against. In 5-player games, the taker calls a King before opening. Explore graphics in our Gallery.
